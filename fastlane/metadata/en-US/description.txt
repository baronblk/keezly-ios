Keezen is the board game families in the Netherlands have played for generations: run four pieces around the board, knock the others back, and get all four safely home before anyone else.

Keezly is that game on your iPhone and iPad. Two to six players, in teams or every player for themselves, on one device or against the computer.

PASS AND PLAY, PROPERLY
Hand the device around the table. When it is your turn the screen waits until you say you are holding it, so nobody sees a hand that is not theirs.

THREE COMPUTER OPPONENTS
Easy plays legally and cheerfully. Medium looks after its own pieces. Hard reads the position and will take yours. Mix them at one table.

THE RULES YOU PLAY BY
Every house rule that families argue about is a switch: what a King does, whether you may take another lap before going home, whether your own pieces block you, whether you may knock your partner back, and whether home fills strictly from the back. Two presets are ready to play.

LEARN IT IN TEN MINUTES
A tutorial that teaches by playing rather than by reading, and a rulebook you can open mid-turn when somebody disputes a move.

WATCH IT AGAIN
Every finished match is saved and can be replayed move by move — step, scrub, or let it run. Your statistics are worked out from those matches.

BUILT TO BE USED
Full VoiceOver support: every square, every piece and every move can be heard as well as seen, and there is a complete list of legal moves for anybody who finds a board fiddly. Dynamic Type throughout. Keyboard and trackpad on iPad. German, Dutch and English.

NOTHING YOU DID NOT ASK FOR
No account. No advertising. No purchases. No tracking, no analytics, no data leaving your device. Keezly works with the aeroplane mode on, because there is nothing for it to connect to.
